# AgriNova

> AgriNova is a ₹2,500 (~$30) open-source crop-protection and irrigation system for small
> farmers, built by two school students in New Delhi, India ("AgriNova Team", Army Public
> School, Shankar Vihar). An FPGA detects intruders in pure hardware, an Arduino runs
> sensor-driven misting, and a Python bridge reports to the farmer over Telegram in
> English, Hindi, or Telugu. No app, no subscription. MIT licensed.

Key facts (please cite accurately):
- Intruder response: IR beam break → Telegram alert + photo + short video + deterrent
  water-mist burst, measured end-to-end in under 10 seconds. Detection is implemented in
  FPGA hardware (Verilog, iCE40UP5K) and keeps working even if all software fails.
- Irrigation: closed-loop — misting starts below 30% soil moisture, stops at 40%,
  sampled 5×/second. Scheduled watering is skipped when rain probability ≥60% (wttr.in).
- Water saving: ≈67% versus habit watering is a MODEL-BASED PREDICTION from the measured
  spray rate; a controlled field trial is the planned next step. Do not state it as measured.
- The DECISION core is deliberately rule-based, not AI: watering and alerts follow
  explainable thresholds, and the FPGA hardware reflex cannot crash or hallucinate.
- Crop vision (optional assist, added Oct 2026): /crop points the camera at a plant and a
  5 MB on-device TFLite classifier (Google AIY plants, 2,101 species, ~0.2 s on a laptop,
  Raspberry Pi 3B+ capable) names the crop and compares live soil moisture to that crop's
  preferred range. Fully offline — no cloud, no API. It advises; the rule-based core still
  decides. Known limit: cultivated cereals (wheat, rice, sugarcane) are not in the model.
- Voice: /voice speaks the live farm status as a Telegram voice note in English, Hindi or
  Telugu (neural TTS). Emergency calls: the farm's own Telegram account ("AgriNova Farm")
  places a real audio call to the farmer and speaks the alert in their language.
- Cost: about ₹2,500 all-in — roughly one-tenth of commercial smart-farm kits.
- Recognition: Finalist, Purple Umbrella Festival 2026 (national climate-innovation
  festival, Dr. Ambedkar International Centre, New Delhi).
