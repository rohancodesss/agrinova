Reflex. Hands. Brain.

Safety-critical detection in hardware; intelligence in software.
⚡ The reflex — FPGA (iCE40UP5K).

Pure hardware watches the IR beam. Reacts instantly, ignores repeats for 10 seconds, and cannot crash, hang, or look away — even if every computer fails.

🦾 The hands — Arduino UNO.

Reads soil moisture and the climate sensor every second, and switches the relay that powers the water-mist pump.

🧠 The brain — Python bridge.

Runs the Telegram bot, camera, weather checks, logs and graphs — in English, Hindi and Telugu. Built for ~Rs 2,500, fully open source.

🙅 Where's the AI?

We chose not to use it. Threshold decisions need rules, not learning — rules are explainable, run without internet, and never hallucinate. Responsible use of AI is knowing when not to use it.
